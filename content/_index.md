# Stay Focused. Stay Safe. Save Lives.

Every time someone gets behind the wheel, their attention matters. A quick glance at a text message, a phone notification, or a conversation can take a driver's focus away from the road. Distracted driving puts drivers, passengers, pedestrians, and other road users at risk.

Our Group 5 project explores the dangers of distracted driving and introduces a proposed solution designed to help drivers keep their attention where it belongs: on the road.

## Why Distracted Driving Matters

Distracted driving is more than just looking at a phone. It includes anything that takes your eyes, hands, or mind away from driving.

- **Texting and driving:** Reading or sending a message can take your eyes off the road.
- **Phone notifications:** Calls, alerts, and social media can tempt drivers to look away.
- **Passenger distractions:** Conversations and activities inside the vehicle can interrupt concentration.
- **Other activities:** Eating, adjusting music, or using navigation can also create risks.

Even a moment of distraction can have serious consequences.

## Meet Our Proposed Solution: Driving Lock

What if drivers had help resisting the temptation to use their phones while driving?

Our proposed **Driving Lock** concept is designed to reduce phone-related distractions by limiting access to distracting phone activities while a vehicle is being driven.

The goal is simple: encourage safer habits, reduce unnecessary phone use, and help drivers focus on driving.

Explore our website to learn how the concept is intended to work and why distracted driving deserves attention.
