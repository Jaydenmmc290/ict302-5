## Parents text and drive too

It's not just teens, 20% of parents admit to texting while driving, and 30% of teens say they've seen their parents do it (Liberty Mutual,2018). The risks are real at any age, and kids notice when adults break the rules.

## Leading by example

Teens look to their parents for cues about what's acceptable. by consistently driving distraction free and keeping your own phone out of reach, you set the standard for safer habits. Your actions can influence your child's attitude toward driving for years to come.

## Making it a family rule

Consider making distraction free driving rule for everyone in the family. Participating together and reminding each other to be distraction free can lead to a united front that helps reinforce the message and keeps everyone safer on the road.
