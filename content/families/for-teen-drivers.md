## Why this matters to you

Distracted driving is a leading cause of accidents among teens. As a new driver, it is easy to underestimate how quickly a glance at your phone can change your life. Staying focused on the road protects your independence and keeps you and others on the road safe.

## What the lock does and doesn't share

The phone lock is designed to keep you safe and not to spy on you. It only uses your phone's movement and connection to your car to tell if you're driving. It does not share your messages, contacts, private data, or where you've been. In an emergency, key features like calls remain available. Your privacy and independence are respected while making the roads safer for everyone.

## Staying in touch with friends

You do not have to lose connection, just safely delay it. Encourage your friends to respect your focus when you are behind the wheel, and let them know you will respond when safely parked. True friends will support your commitment to safety and may follow your lead.
