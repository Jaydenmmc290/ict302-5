## Your role

As a parent, you are both a teacher and a rule enforcer when it comes to safe driving. Teens look to you for guidance, so modeling distraction-free habits and setting clear expectations about phone use behind the wheel are crucial steps in keeping them safe.

## From noticing to acting

Many parents notice risky behaviors, but hesitate to address them. Studies show there's often a gap between seeing concerning habits and stepping in, whether from uncertainty or wanting to respect independence (Mott, 2026). Speaking up early and consistently makes a real difference. 

## Peace of mind

Using a phone lock system can relieve the stress of constantly worrying about your teen's choices on the road. It helps to create a safer driving environment while showing your teen you trust them to make smart decision and giving everyone in the family a greater peace of mind.
