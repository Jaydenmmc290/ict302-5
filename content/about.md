## The project

Our mission is to make the roads that we use everyday, safer for all. This starts with identifying good habits from those who are most likely to overlook the dangers of the road. Pew Research Center (2024) found that nearly half of teens ages 13-17 were online almost constantly. However, we know that this problem doesn't just exist with the younger generations. Parents are both the teachers and rule enforcers, if the parents refuse to adapt these new habits, then teens are quick to follow.

Our solution to this problem is disabling the ability to access distracting information when behind the wheel all together. We have built a driving lock that is built directly into the phone operating system, this makes sure you never have to access an app to ensure your safety. Research shows that blocking works when people use it. With our design there is no need to worry about turning your phone into driving mode, it has already been done for you.
