## Worry behind the wheel

More than half of parents-about 51%-worry that their teen will use a phone while driving (Jewett et al., Nationwide 2025). The dangers of distracted driving are a top concern, with parents fearing accidents, missed messages, and the temptation of constant connectivity.

## Riding with other teen drivers

Parents aren't only concerned when their child is driving. Many also worry about teens riding with friends who might use their phones behind the wheel. The Mott report found that a significant number of teens have been passengers in cars where the driver was distracted.

## The gap between seeing and acting

Even when parents and teens notice unsafe driving, not everyone steps in. according to the Mott 2026 report, Over half of the parents have seen risky behavior, but only 1 in 4 take direct action. Open conversations and clear expectations can help close this gap and make everyone safer on the road.
