## Passengers keep their phones

Passengers in the car will not have to lock their phone. The phone lock system only activates when it detects that you are the one driving the car. Passengers can use their phone as usual, so nobody loses access out of convenience.

## Riding in your own car

Sometimes, you might ride as passenger in your own car if your parent or friend is driving. In those cases, you can unlock your phone by entering a special override code confirming that you are not the driver. This keeps the system flexible for real life situations.

## How overrides are recorded

Each time someone uses the override code, the event is recorded for accountability. This helps ensure the system is being used honestly, and lets parents or guardians see when and why overrides happen. It's a balance between privacy, trust, and safe driving.
