## About the driving lock

Q: Will the lock block phones for passengers too?

No the lock only activates on the phone that's connected as the driver. Passengers can use their phones as usual.

Q: Can I still use my phone in an emergency?

Yes, emergency call features remain fully accessible, even when the driving lock is on.

Q: Does it work with any car?

The system works with cars compatible with CarPlay, Android Auto, or Bluetooth connectivity.

## For teens

Q: Can I use maps while driving?

Yes, navigation apps are available on your car display so you can get directions safely.

Q: What if I'm riding in my own car but not driving?

You can unlock your phone by entering a passenger override code. Every override is logged for accountability.

Q: What does the lock share about me?

The lock only tracks your phone's movement to know you're driving. It never shares tour messages, contacts, or location details.

## For parents

Q: What can I see on my teen's trip summary?

You will see whether the lock stayed active, if any overrides were used, and which features were accessed during a drive.

Q: How do I set up the driving lock for my teen?

You can use iOS or Android family controls to require the lock on your teen's device and monitor driving safety together.

Q: Is this system just for teens?

No, anyone who wants to avoid distractions while driving can use the system for added safety.
